# NEXORA MARKET — Launch Disclosures Draft

These notes are product disclosures, not legal advice and not a substitute for a reviewed privacy policy or marketplace terms.

## Privacy note

The app is designed to store account/profile data, carts, wishlists, seller applications, product listings, and COD order data in the connected Supabase project. Delivery name, phone number, and address are collected only for a COD order request. Users should not enter passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, or unnecessary sensitive information into marketplace text fields. A formal privacy contact and retention/deletion process are not configured yet.

## Terms note

Live listings must represent genuine inventory and accurate prices, stock, descriptions, categories, and images. The demo preview is separated from live data and cannot create a real order. COD is the only enabled payment method; no online payment or card collection is active. An order is a request recorded in the database and should not be treated as fulfilled until the operator confirms it. A reviewed marketplace terms document is still required before unrestricted public selling.

## Seller rules draft

A seller must control or be authorized to sell each listed item, provide truthful listing details, keep price and stock current, use HTTPS image URLs, and not upload misleading, unlawful, unsafe, or infringing content. Seller approval does not guarantee sales. Administrators may reject applications, keep drafts unpublished, archive listings, or suspend access under the final reviewed marketplace rules.

## Delivery and returns disclosure

No delivery partner, service area, delivery fee, delivery timeline, return window, refund process, damaged-item process, or COD refusal process is configured in this release. The app must not promise any of those items until the operator defines and publishes them for the actual operating area.

## Contact disclosure

No business support email, phone number, address, or staffed customer-support channel has been configured. The public GitHub repository can receive technical issue reports, but it is not a substitute for a customer-support channel. A real marketplace launch requires the operator to add and monitor an official contact method.

## Current launch status

- Live catalog: empty; no genuine inventory has been published.
- Authentication: implemented, but no confirmed QA account is available for end-to-end testing.
- Admin role: no admin role record is currently assigned.
- Payments: online payments disabled; COD only.
- Security: Supabase Leaked Password Protection remains disabled and must be enabled from the Supabase Dashboard before account onboarding.
- Launch decision: controlled configuration only; not unrestricted public selling yet.
